## A) Vendor / Farmer Flyer

### Headline
**Grow Local. Get Found. Sell More — No Middleman.**

### Subhead
MyStandMap helps local growers get discovered by nearby shoppers looking for fresh produce, eggs, honey, and more.

### What MyStandMap is
MyStandMap is a local-first platform that connects your stand directly to people in your area who want to buy from farmers.

### Why growers should use it (boxed benefits)
- **Be discovered locally** by shoppers already searching for farm stands
- **Claim or create your stand** in minutes
- **Update hours and offerings** as seasons change
- **Build trust** with clear listing details and direct visibility
- **Keep it direct** — no middleman between you and your customers

### How it works (3 steps)
1. **Sign up** for your free account
2. **Claim or create** your stand listing
3. **Add details**: location, schedule, products, contact info

### What to prepare before signup (checklist)
- Stand name and location/address
- Seasonal hours/days open
- Product list (what you sell)
- Contact method (phone, text, or email)
- Optional stand photos

## 4) Canva layout direction (final-quality, print-ready)

Use **US Letter 8.5 x 11 in, portrait** for both flyers.

### Shared layout spec
- Tight vertical rhythm (reduced white space)
- Bold headline bar at top (high contrast)
- 2-column middle section for benefits + process/checklist
- Distinct boxed modules with subtle corner radius
- Strong bottom CTA strip with QR + large typed URL
- Font sizing for older-audience readability (body around 12–14 pt, key CTA larger)
- Prioritize clean sans-serif typography and strong contrast

### Suggested section stack (top to bottom)
1. Brand header + logo
2. Hero headline + subhead
3. “What it is” explainer block
4. Benefits block
5. “How it works” numbered strip
6. Secondary block (Vendor: signup prep checklist / Consumer: why local matters)
7. Full-width CTA strip with QR + typed URL
